\section{}
\subsection{Multipath propagation}
One transmitted signal is reflected in some way, so that delayed versions of that signal all arrive at the receiver.
\subsection{Doppler Effect}
Shift in frequency from what was transmitted, usually caused by velocity, though I think that frequency offsets from clock skew might also be considered Doppler.
\subsection{Diversity}
Sending data across different frequencies or across time.
\subsection{Equalization}
Reversing the channel effects to recover a less distorted signal, used to recover signal power in fading channels
\subsection{Spread Spectrum}
Spreading signal power over time or frequency.
\subsection{MIMO}
Multi-in multi-out, the use of multiple antenna elements usually to do beamforming
\subsection{FDMA, OFDMA, TDMA, CDMA}
Frequency/Orthogonal Frequency, Time, Cell Division Multiple Access. Different ways of supporting multiple users on the same frequency bands.
